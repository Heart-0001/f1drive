
################ ai press fix

== summary
Fixed the Monaco lap-1 chicane bump without losing round 3's passing. Nothing is committed.

Cause: round 3 kept pressing a slower car into the braking zone without allowing for its braking. On lap 1 two or three cars reached the chicane together and the next car attacked one of them mid-chicane. Reproduced in Electron (bots.js part pits: 7 of 40 runs with round 3's file, always Bortoleto and Alonso, 0.158-0.203 at samples 316-319, 17.1 s after lights out) and in node (new devtests/ai-test/press.js: same pair, same place, 19 of 160 runs, max 0.34; the file before round 3 and pressing off both 0 of 48).

js/ai.js now (decisions recorded in devtests/ai-test/README.md):
1. The press runs fully until 2.5 s before the car ahead's braking point and is gone 1 s before it; it also stops while that car slows by more than 2-6 m/s2. The braking point comes from a new per-profile table of corner minima (G.apex), computed once per step.
2. Once released, the gap is held: no dropping back, no closing in. Its braking is reacted to only as far as this car is not already braking as hard.
3. The pass is set up instead: in the 2.5-1 s window a known slower car is attacked on the inside of the coming corner; not alongside by turn-in, the attack is given up. No attack starts mid-corner or within 2.5 s of a corner tighter than full lock.
4. A parked car is also checked where the car's line is heading (a car returning to the line after letting someone by hit one at 0.50).
5. F1.AI.warmUp runs an extra 12 s train into the slowest corner, keeping first-race memory at round 3's level.
6. New diagnostic field ai.state.press.

The literal version you suggested (drop back to the normal gap, react fully to braking) removed the bump but made reversed-grid finishing order worse than the pre-round-3 file (0.23 vs 0.29).

Results (pre-round-3 / round 3 / now). passing.js seeds 0-5, 168 races: passes 727 / 959 / 1076; finish order vs skill, mean of per-seed medians, all 0.573 / 0.668 / 0.667, reversed grid 0.472 / 0.668 / 0.675, random grid 0.598 / 0.685 / 0.667; contacts 31 (10 over 0.25) / 16 (0) / 12 (1); offs 220 / 112 / 38. matrix.js single seed: passes 115 / 166 / 187, order 0.74 / 0.79 / 0.72 (lower on one seed, level over six), contacts 6 / 4 / 3, offs 30 / 23 / 4. Electron bots.js part pits: 40 of 40 runs 14/14, no chicane contact on lap 1. Full bots.js: 193/193 once, 192/193 once (part monaco's 93 % tyre-wear check, which also fails on round 3's file in 2 of 8 Monaco runs vs 1 of 8 now). Unit tests: all 36 pass, two new ones fail on round 3's file; all 12 node suites pass. Memory: 1.49 B per think() call; first race with warm-up 21.5 MB (round 3 22.6). Driving fingerprint changes as expected; warm=1 prints the same lines.
