
################ Monaco tunnel

== summary
I finished the tunnel module. Monaco's tunnel is now built in code, and four other circuits also get the short covered stretches that the OpenStreetMap data supports. I edited no existing project file and did not commit.

**Data (tools/tunnels.json, built by devtests/tunnel-test/make-data.js; js/tunnels.js holds an identical copy, and make-data.js --check confirms they match).** All 40 circuits were fetched from OpenStreetMap on 2026-10-01 and checked: every circuit is recorded with what was found, and nothing is left undecided.
- **Monaco tunnel (mc-1929):** OSM ways 4230891 and 1230247123 (Boulevard Louis II, tunnel=yes). On the game track it runs from sample 760 to 944 (s 1520–1888 m), 368 m long. A road-wiki page puts it at about 400 m.
  - Entry facade is the Fairmont hotel (OSM node 9944534420 lies in the scenery footprint over the road). Exit facade is plain concrete, next to the Auditorium Rainier III (OSM way 112689159).
  - Correction to the task brief: the real tunnel is "partly open on one side along the shoreline" (SGM's lighting project page). It opens beside the walkway, on the sea side, which is the driver's left. I modelled a colonnade on that side for about 190 m, where the scenery data's sea lies 0–16 m beyond the wall. The exit end is enclosed.
- **Monaco, Portier (18 m):** the circuit's own OSM way 1470365907 is tagged tunnel=yes under the new Portier roundabout structure. Confidence is lower than for the tunnel.
- **Monza:** a 24 m underpass below the old banked oval (OSM way 1443867793, covered=yes).
- **Madring:** two short tunnels under the motorway, 68 m and 134 m (OSM ways 87096566/34049751 and 827423913/827423914). A circuit guide confirms the two tunnels.
- **Singapore, Raffles Boulevard:** two passages under buildings. The first is 22 m: clear height 4.5 m, building top 6 m, from OSM way 393090492. The second is 46 m.
- **Yas Marina:** a 28 m passage under the W hotel's bridge: clear height 9 m, bridge top 18 m, from OSM way 1387292013.
- **Left out on purpose:** Monaco's Larvotto road tunnel (it runs below the circuit), Singapore's car-park aisles, and Suzuka's crossover (the game levels it into a flat crossing). Yas Marina's real pit-exit tunnel is also out, because the game's pit lane is on the surface.

**What the module builds:** walls just behind the track's barriers, a flat ceiling 6.8 m above the road that follows its slope, rows of light fixtures, advertising boards, and portal facades. It also fills in the part of a building over the road that scenery.js drops, plus the rest of that building on either side. A road shade makes the tunnel read as a dark hole from outside, and a glare layer brightens the exits seen from inside. The whole thing is 4 draw calls; Monaco is about 10.9k triangles and builds in about 18 ms.

**API:** `F1.buildTunnels(track, trackData, data?)` returns `{group, inTunnel(i), lightAt(i), sceneLight(i, 'hemi'|'sun'), covered(i), update(t, viewIndex, sceneScaled), tunnels, stats, dispose()}`. There is also `F1.tunnelImpulse(sampleRate, opts)`, which generates a reverb impulse response for the sound.

**Tests:**
- devtests/tunnel-test/check.js (node): 68 out of 68 checks pass. In every covered stretch the barrier is hit first and the tunnel wall sits 0.52 m behind it. Nothing from the tunnel is inside the drivable space or over any other part of the track. A car steered into either wall stops at the barrier. No scenery is inside any tunnel.
- devtests/tunnel-test/run.js loads the real game in a muted offscreen Electron window and adds the tunnel through F1.game. It passed 9 of 9 checks on Monaco, Madring, Monza, Singapore and Yas Marina, including an autopilot drive through at 180–290 km/h with no wall contact.
- Measured cost at Monaco: about +0.05–0.07 ms GPU per frame, and +6–8 draw calls including the cockpit mirror passes.

**Screenshots I looked at:**
- Monaco: the approach, entry and inside from the cockpit; the exit seen from inside; the drive sequence; the hotel entry portal; the exit portal; aerial and sea-side views; the car against the barrier.
- The other four circuits: their portals and insides.

Remaining visual issues: the tunnel is 25 m wide because the game puts Monaco's walls 12 m from the centreline, so it looks wider than the real one. The Fairmont's height comes from the scenery data.

== recommendations
 - index.html: add <script src="js/tunnels.js"></script> right after js/scenery.js (before js/main.js). package.json's js/*.js glob already packages it, and the CSP needs no change (canvas textures only).
 - js/main.js initGraphics(): keep references to the lights (hemi = new THREE.HemisphereLight(0xdcebff, 0x556644, 0.85); sun = ... 0.9) and their base values HEMI0 = 0.85, SUN0 = 0.9.
 - js/main.js selectTrack(): after scenery, dispose any old tunnels, then: if (typeof F1.buildTunnels === 'function') { try { tunnels = F1.buildTunnels(track, data); scene.add(tunnels.group); } catch (e) { tunnels = null; console.error(e); } }. Also dispose it wherever scenery is disposed. Expose it as get tunnels() in F1.game.
 - js/main.js frame(), before renderer.render: if (tunnels) { var vi = car.state.sampleIndex; hemi.intensity = HEMI0 * tunnels.sceneLight(vi, 'hemi'); sun.intensity = SUN0 * tunnels.sceneLight(vi, 'sun'); tunnels.update(t / 1000, vi); }. Measured with the harness: the sun goes to 0 inside and the hemisphere light to 0.83 deep in Monaco (the light is dimmed to lightAt^0.4, mimicking eye adaptation). This lights the cockpit and car right; the module raises its own interior brightness and adds glare on the exits. HUD mirrors render afterwards with the same lights, which is fine. Leave fog and exposure alone: the game has no tone mapping, the curved Monaco tunnel limits sight lines to about 160 m (fog starts at 300 m), and the module's glare handles the bright exit. On the menu still render, pass the same index. If main.js does NOT scale the lights, call tunnels.update(t, vi, false) so the road shade stays on.
 - js/audio.js: add setTunnel(k) (or read own.tunnel in update), called each frame from main.js with tunnels.inTunnel(car.state.sampleIndex). Build once: conv = ctx.createConvolver(). Take ir = F1.tunnelImpulse(ctx.sampleRate, {width: 25, height: 6.8, rt60: 1.4}) (or the current stretch's width/height from tunnels.tunnels), copy ir.left / ir.right into a 2-channel AudioBuffer, and set conv.buffer. Route hp -> conv -> tunWet (gain 0.45 * k) -> act, in parallel with the dry path. Add a peaking filter at 450 Hz, Q 0.8, gain 4 * k dB on the dry path, plus about +2.5 dB * k on the engine (reflections) and +3 dB * k on the wind/road roar. Ramp with setTargetAtTime(…, 0.05). The impulse has a 40 ms ceiling flutter and 73 ms side-wall echoes, decays 55 dB by 1.2 s, and is deterministic (checked in check.js). Disconnect the convolver input after k has been 0 for 2 s to save CPU. inTunnel already ramps in over the first 30 m (Portier peaks at 0.12, Monaco's tunnel reaches 1). Verify it offline in devtests/audio-test (RMS and decay with k = 0 vs 1).
 - js/scenery.js (optional, low priority): build the tunnels before the scenery and pass tunnels.covered so trackside furniture is skipped at covered samples. Debris fences, boards, marshal posts and city poles stand just outside the tunnel block (fence 6 cm outside it; 2 fence triangles inside the block's solid part at Monaco; all hidden) and never inside the tunnel (check.js: 0 at all 5 circuits). No change is needed for buildings: the footprints that span the road (Fairmont #9/#10/#15, Portier #17, Singapore #15) are dropped by fitPoly, and tunnels.js rebuilds their mass.
 - js/track.js: no change needed. The car hits the barrier at wallPosDist / wallNegDist, and the tunnel wall sits 0.52 m behind it, only where wallPos / wallNeg is set. check.js measured 0 bad rays over about 3,000 per circuit (max face error 7 cm, from 2 m chords on the 15 m Portier corner), and car.js never got past the barrier face. Optional: export each sample's barrier outer offset so the tunnel can sit exactly behind barriers that track.js makes thinner than 0.5 m; the possible gap of up to 0.3 m is invisible from the road.
 - .gitignore (owner's call): either add devtests/tunnel-test/cache/ (2.2 MB of OSM responses, about 10 min to refetch; make-data.js --check skips circuits whose cache is missing) or commit it so tools/tunnels.json can be rebuilt fully offline. devtests/tunnel-test/out/ is already ignored by devtests/*/out/.
 - devtests/README.md: add the tunnel harness. 'node devtests/tunnel-test/check.js' (68 checks, about 10 s, no network); 'npx electron devtests/tunnel-test/run.js' (muted; env TRACK, TAG, ONLY=shots|drive|perf, MODE=rec|off, WHICH=n; about 1 min per circuit); 'node devtests/tunnel-test/make-data.js [--check]' (data builder).

== openIssues
 - Correction to the task brief: the brief said the real tunnel has no opening to the harbour side. The sources say it is partly open along the shoreline (SGM's lighting project page; the hotel stands on pillars over the sea, with a walkway on the sea side). I modelled about 190 m of colonnade on the left (sea side), placed where the scenery data's water comes within 16 m. Nobody here has checked it against race onboard footage. If the user prefers it fully enclosed, remove the 'open' line from DECISIONS in make-data.js and rerun it.
 - Monaco Portier underpass (18 m) and the Singapore and Yas Marina passages rely on OSM tags alone (Portier mapped from 2023-12 imagery, after the Portier Cove works). Confidence is lower than for the tunnel; each decision is written out with its OSM way ids in tools/tunnels.json under 'sources'.
 - The tunnel is 25 m wide wall to wall, wider than the real one (about 12–14 m), because track.js puts Monaco's walls 12 m from the centreline. It cannot be narrower without moving the collision walls. The hotel's height (top about 14.7 m above the road) comes from the scenery data's footprint heights (9.6 / 12.5 m), not from the real Fairmont.
 - None of main.js, audio.js, scenery.js or index.html is wired up yet; the recommendations give the exact integration. Until then the harness patches the module into the page.
 - The reverb impulse is tested numerically only; nobody has listened to it, and all harness windows were muted.
 - devtests/tunnel-test/cache/ (2.2 MB, 40 new files) is untracked and not ignored. The owner should decide between committing and ignoring it (see recommendations).
