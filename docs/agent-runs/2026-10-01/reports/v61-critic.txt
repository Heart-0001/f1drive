
################ v6.1 critic + re-run

== summary
I played v6.1 in muted offscreen Electron windows using real keys, mouse clicks and a fake pad, and looked at the screenshots. I found and fixed two real problems, plus one doc/code mismatch in js/hudmirrors.js. Every suite on the list passes on the final tree.

**Fixed**
1. **Light curtains filled the mirrors.** Right after driving through a pit-lane light curtain, it covered both HUD mirrors (and the in-car left mirror) for about a second: amber at the entry, green at the exit. At the exit 51–64 % of the mirror area was green, exactly when the car rejoins the traffic. js/main.js now marks the track's `pitCurtains` mesh `userData.mirror = false`, so both the HUD mirrors and the in-car mirrors leave the curtains out. Measured after the fix: 0 % at the entry and at the exit (pit-d-after-exit-0s.png).
2. **A battery note described behaviour the game does not have.** The note on the 2014 Red Bull, Toro Rosso, Lotus and Caterham cards said "按加速鈕常常沒反應" (the boost button often does nothing). In the game E / A always works; these cars only deploy less (0.88×). It now reads "ERS 軟體與可靠度問題多，電能部署偏弱". I edited tools/ers-data.json and regenerated js/seasons-data.js with `node tools/build-cars.mjs`; only those 4 notes changed.
3. **Mirror minimum width mismatch.** `F1.hudMirrorsLayout` in js/hudmirrors.js used a 224 px minimum glass width; index.html uses 176 px. The module now uses 176 px too, which also shrinks the mirrors at 443 px wide or less, matching the CSS.

**Checked and working as asked**
- **First boot:** opens on 2026 with the 2026 standard car; the key map has the 後照鏡 / V column and the pad row.
- **Mirror switch and V:**
  - V toggles the mirrors with the toast 後照鏡：開／關（V）, and the boxes move back up when they are off.
  - Holding V down toggles only once. V does nothing in the menu or while typing a name.
  - The 設定 switch is remembered after a restart.
- **Overtaking in a real two-window room:** the overtaking car shows in the correct mirror. A car passing on your right shows in your right mirror, and once it is ahead you show in its left mirror. Name tags are not drawn in the mirrors.
- **Layout:** with the start lights plus a toast, and with the results overlay plus a toast, nothing covers the mirrors at 1280×720, 1920×1080, 1100, 1000, 800 and 700 px wide.
- **Pad:**
  - A (and RB) held with RT while steering deploys the battery every frame (+57 km/h after 6 s), and the stick still steers. A deploys nothing while braking.
  - B and LB toggle the limiter once, even when both are pressed together. X cycles the compound with a toast; Y resets onto the centreline; View toggles the racing line; Start opens and closes the menu.
  - In the pit lane the prompts say 請開啟限速器（B） and 按 B 關閉限速器.
- **Battery per car** (Monza straight, same frames with and without the battery):
  - 2014 Mercedes gains +26.3 km/h; the 2015 McLaren-Honda gains +21.0 km/h.
  - 2011 HRT: no battery bar, a 無 KERS chip, and E or A changes nothing.
  - 2026 Aston Martin gains the least after 2–3 s of E (+32.7 / +46.6 km/h against +35.5–36.7 / +50.4–52.3 for the others). By the end of the straight every 2026 car is about +29–30 km/h, because the 290–345 km/h deploy fade (the 2026 regulation, built into car.js) caps them all, so the weakest battery only shows earlier in the straight.

**Re-run on the final tree** (exact numbers in testsRun): all 11 node suites, build-cars --check, the 4 gamepad tests, ui-gp, ui-v6, solo, solo-v6, hudmirrors-test, v6-smoke, gp-smoke, mp-e2e, v6-critic, online, online-v6, and the new v61-critic (98/98). Nothing committed, pushed or packaged.

== bugsFound
 - js/main.js (fixed): a pit-lane light curtain just driven through filled the HUD mirrors and the in-car left mirror with amber (at the entry) or green (at the exit) for about 1 s. At the exit it covered 51–64 % of the mirror area right as the car rejoins traffic. Screenshot devtests/v61-critic/out/pit-d-after-exit-0s.png before the fix; now 0 %.
 - tools/ers-data.json / js/seasons-data.js (fixed): the 2014 Renault-powered cars' battery note '按加速鈕常常沒反應' told players the button often does nothing, which never happens in the game (deploy is just 0.88×).
 - js/hudmirrors.js (fixed): the built-in fallback layout used a 224 px minimum glass width while index.html uses 176 px.

== openIssues
 - Blind spot, not changed: a car 6 m to the side (the other grid column) is visible in the HUD mirror until about 10 m behind, and in the main view only from about 5 m ahead. In the room test it disappeared for roughly 1.5 s while alongside (room-04-A-pass-05..12). With 3 m lateral offset the gap is about 7 m. This is like real mirrors. Widening the mirrors (hfov / yaw in js/hudmirrors.js) or adding a 'car alongside' indicator would be a design decision for the user.
 - 2026 battery: the Aston Martin's weaker deploy shows in the first 2–3 s of E and in lap time (+0.16 % in ers-effect.json). At the end of a long straight every 2026 car gains about the same (+29–30 km/h) because of the 290–345 km/h fade (FIA rule, in js/car.js). Nothing changed.
 - Pre-existing, cosmetic, not changed: a toast that wraps to two lines overlaps the top 10 px of the start-lights box from 721 to 1000 px wide (with or without mirrors). The lamps themselves are not covered.
 - Docs still to update (orchestrator / stage K): devtests/README.md row for v61-critic (text in apiNotes); hudmirrors-test is now 41 checks / about 70 s; v6-smoke is 144 checks; js/README-interfaces.md v6.1 controls / mirrors notes as listed by the glue stage, plus 'pit curtains carry userData.mirror = false'.
 - Defaults to record in docs/v6-plan.md: curtains left out of all mirrors; the 2014 Renault battery note reworded; mirror field of view unchanged.
