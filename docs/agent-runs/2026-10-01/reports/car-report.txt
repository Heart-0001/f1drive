
################ car.js + gamepad.js

== summary
I implemented the v6 car in js/car.js and the new pad buttons in js/gamepad.js, and proved the golden rule.

- **Golden rule:** in test/car.test.js the new F1.createCar() (tyres at wear rate 0, no boost or limiter) replays recorded runs of the v5 car (devtests/car-v6/car-v5.js, own vm sandbox). That is 48 runs on 6 tracks: launch, grid launch on the pad, braking and reverse, slalom with an R reset, walls, grass, a racing-line lap on keys and one on the pad. All 134,483 steps and 1,613,796 state values are bit-identical (max difference 0).
- **Harnesses match v5 byte for byte:** drive-all, raceline drive, ap-sim, laps drive and targeted give the same output with js/car.js as with the v5 file. F1.CAR_PERF = F1.carPerf(REF_SPEC), and every v5 field and function value is identical.
- **What was added:** REF_SPEC and carPerf with sanitised specs; gear with hysteresis, rpm and shiftT; ERS tuned on the real tracks; the pit limiter; tyres fed every physics substep with grip scaling, slip, vib and slow puncture drag; the pit lane (asphalt, a solid pit wall from both sides and at both ends, no global re-locate in the boxes).
- **Pit lane on all 40 tracks:** 640 lane visits (16 boxes each) at the limiter speed, with no contact or grass, every stop within 0.02 m along and 0.12 m across. Fuzzing (3 seeds, 41,600 runs, 39M steps, 2.3M pit-wall contact steps) never got through the pit wall or garage face. A copy with the pit wall disabled fails loudly: 19,471 inside-wall steps and 547 crossings.
- **Gamepad:** RB or B held = boost, LB = pressed.limiter, Back = pressed.compound, also on the raw (non-standard) layout. mergeInput merges boost and passes keys.limiter through.
- **Real game:** it cannot boot as-is, because index.html already loads js/seasons-data.js and js/cars.js, which do not exist yet. Through a wrapper that drops those two tags, gp-smoke ONLY=free,solo passes 54/55. The one failure is a stale ui.js storage check, not the car. My own game check (devtests/car-v6/game-check.js) passes 13/13, and I looked at its screenshots.

== apiNotes
 - F1.REF_SPEC: sanitised copy of the internal reference. id '2025-standard', year 2025, team/teamZh 'F1Drive', car '標準賽車', engine '1.6 L V6 渦輪混合動力', colours '#9AA0A6' / '#2B2F36' (the cars-data standard car), ratings all 50, note ''. Physics are exactly the v5 constants: power 970.1389 W/kg, dragK 0.0012, downforce 0.00220725, latBase 20, latMax 44, brakeBase 12, traction 11. gearKmh [60,100,140,180,220,260,300], topKmh 345, rpm 4000/11800/12500, shiftTime 0.05, 6 cylinders, 'hybrid', ers {store 4656.67 J/kg, power 145.52 W/kg, harvest 230 W/kg}, cockpit 'halo18'. DEVIATION: README says cockpit 'halo'; I followed the task text ('halo18', which also fits a 2025 car under the 2022-on rule).
 - Addition F1.sanitizeSpec(spec) -> a new object with exactly the contract's CarSpec fields (unknown fields are dropped). Rules: physics numbers accepted within 1/4..4x the reference, ERS numbers within 1/10..10x; gearKmh 1..11 strictly increasing values in 10..500; topKmh in (last gear + 1)..600; rpmIdle 500..20000, rpmShift >= idle+100, rpmMax >= shift, all <= 25000; shiftTime 0.01..0.2; cylinders even 2..16; aspiration 'na'|'hybrid'; cockpit 'modern'|'halo'|'halo18'; id /^[a-z0-9-]{1,40}$/; year an integer 1950..2100; colours #rrggbb; ratings 0..100; strings cut at 120 chars. ers null/false = no battery, missing = the reference ERS, bad fields = reference values. Anything else invalid takes the reference value.
 - F1.carPerf(spec) -> perf. It has every F1.CAR_PERF field and function, in the v5 key order and with the same meaning, computed from the sanitised spec. Derived values: muLat = latBase/g, brakeDrag = BRAKE_DRAG*(dragK/0.0012), latAero and brakeAero consistent with them. Added after the v5 keys: gearFor(v, currentGear?) (no current gear: plain table; 1..n given: hysteresis, downshift 8 km/h below the lower gear's upshift speed; -1 below -0.5 m/s, 0 below 0.5 m/s); rpmFor(v, gear?) (gear defaults to gearFor(v); reverse uses gear 1's ratio; 0 -> rpmIdle; clamped to idle..max); gears (n); topSpeed (power balances dragK v^2 + roll, capped where traction runs out; exactly 330/3.6 for the reference); topSpeedBoost (deploying: 346.2 km/h for the reference); ers; spec. F1.CAR_PERF = F1.carPerf(REF): every v5 number and function output is identical (Object.is on a 3888-point grid). perf.maxAccel does NOT include ERS.
 - F1.createCar(spec?, opts?): spec defaults to REF_SPEC. opts.tyres = an instance, or false for none; otherwise F1.createTyres({random: opts.random}) is used when js/tyres.js is loaded (looked up at createCar time), else car.tyres = null (grip 1, exactly the v5 car; the node devtests load car.js without tyres.js). car = {state, reset, update, setSpec, setBattery, spec, perf, tyres}. setSpec(spec) replaces car.spec (sanitised copy) and car.perf, keeps the battery charge (0 if the new spec has no ERS), and recomputes gear and rpm. setBattery(v): clamped 0..1, ignored if not a number, always 0 without ERS. A new car starts with battery 1. reset() leaves the battery and the tyres alone and sets gear 0, rpm idle, throttle/brake/deploy/harvest/slip 0.
 - input adds boost (bool, hold) and limiter (bool). state adds, refreshed every update: throttle, brake (the pedals after key/analog merge, raw: both can be > 0); gear (-1 | 0 | 1..n; standing: 1 with the throttle >= the brake, -1 with the brake (the car reverses), else 0); rpm; shiftT (s since the last change, 9 before the first); shiftDir (+1/-1 for any gear change including N->1 and 1->R, initially +1); battery, deploy, harvest (0..1, means over the update: deploy = delivered ERS power / ers.power, harvest = stored power / ers.harvest); slip; limiter (= input.limiter); limitKmh (addition: the limiter speed in use, track.pit.limitKmh or 80); inPit (= track.pit.inLane(sampleIndex, d)); vib (= car.tyres.state.vib, else 0). The drive is never interrupted by a gear change: the launch speed trace is bit-identical to v5.
 - ERS model (the contract left the details to me; one deviation). Deploy happens when boost, throttle > 0, brake pedal exactly 0, battery > 0, v > 0.5, and the limiter is not fading the drive. It adds (min(tyreCap, iceFull + ers.power/v) - iceFull) * throttle, where iceFull = min(traction, P/v) and tyreCap = muTraction * a_n * grip.traction (on grass the grass cap). DEVIATION: it is limited by the tyres' friction, not by the 11 m/s^2 TRACTION drivetrain cap. With that cap ERS would do nothing below 317 km/h, because the reference car is traction-capped up to there. Consequence: at a standing start boost adds only what the tyres allow (0-100 km/h 2.58 s instead of 2.73). The battery is drained by the power actually delivered.
 - ERS harvest (same design freedom): harvest = min(ers.harvest, 0.38 x brake deceleration x pedal x speed), i.e. a share of the braking power, proportional to the pedal and growing with speed. Lift-off (throttle 0, brake 0, above 20 m/s) harvests 0.12 x ers.harvest. Nothing is harvested while deploying or with a full battery. It is bookkeeping only: braking with ERS is bit-identical to a car with ers null.
 - ERS measured with the real car on real tracks (node devtests/car-v6/ers.js [all]). Store: empty after 32.0 s from 200 or 300 km/h (34.7 s from a standing start, where the tyres limit delivery). Top speed deploying 346.2 km/h (330 without).
 - ERS straight gain: at full throttle from the straight's start to where the no-boost lap starts braking, deploying gains Monza +17.4 (1114 m), Spa +16.9, Silverstone +19.0, Suzuka +18.6 km/h; every straight of 900 m or more on all 40 tracks gains +16.3..19.7 (mean 18.1). On a flat strip from 150 km/h: 600 m +23.7, 900 m +19.3, 1200 m +17.2.
 - ERS harvest per racing lap without deploying, empty battery, analog autopilot (the calibration driver): Monza 55 %, Monaco 58 %, Spa 65 %, Suzuka 63 %, Silverstone 58 %. The keyboard line driver, which always brakes at 100 % in bursts: 48 / 80 / 70 / 73 / 63 %. All 40 tracks, pad: 40..84 %; 30 tracks are within 50..70, stop-and-go tracks such as Sochi 84, Bahrain 81 and Singapore 79 are above, the fastest (Indianapolis 40, Interlagos 44, Watkins Glen 49) below.
 - ERS lap time: deploying whenever the throttle is full above 100 km/h, battery management by harvest only, laps 2-4 of a stint on the keyboard line driver, gains Monza 1.86 s, Monaco 2.68, Spa 2.34, Silverstone 1.94, Suzuka 2.49; 1.32..3.96 s over all 40 tracks (mean 2.47).
 - Pit limiter: while input.limiter, the drive is multiplied by clamp((limit - v) / 0.5 m/s, 0, 1). It never brakes (entering at 150 km/h it coasts bit-identically to a car with no throttle). It holds 79.82 km/h (59.9 on 60 km/h lanes) and never goes above the limit. Limit = track.pit.limitKmh, or 80 when there is no pit or no track.
 - Tyres: car.tyres.update(h, load) is called every physics SUBSTEP, where load = {speed, lat (share of the tyre-scaled lateral grip in use, sign = turn, capped at 1), brake (the pedal when v > 0.5, the throttle when braking while reversing), drive (|drive| / traction, capped at 1), slip = min(1, 2*(latReq/latMax - 1)) (the x2 scale the tyres owner asked for; lateral only, the car model has no wheelspin or lock-up), onGrass, hit (the strongest impact so far in this update)}. The grip multipliers scale latMax (friction and saturation cap), the traction limit and the braking deceleration, all whole, as the tyres owner's calibration patch did. A puncture adds a resistance of (1.0 + 0.0043 v^2) m/s^2 phased in over 0.5 s: about 150 km/h flat out on a front puncture, about 100 km/h on a rear; lateral grip x0.74 means half the racing line's pace is clean. fit() cures it.
 - Pit lane: onGrass = |d| > halfW && !pit.paved(i, d) (the track owner's addition, tapers included). The pit wall is solid where pit.wallD(i) is finite: the car's origin stays at least wallHalfT + 1 m from its centre line. At its two ends the zone is extended by the car's reach along the lane (2.6 m nose/tail, 1 m sideways, by heading). The car is pushed out through the face its path crossed last (else the shortest way), with the outer walls' impact, friction and heading swing (state.hit). In the pit complex (from..to, pit side, off the road) the located sample may be up to 40 m away without a global re-locate; nothing changes elsewhere. The garage face is the samples' wall distance, as before.
 - gamepad.js: state.boost = RB (5) or B (1) held. state.pressed.limiter = LB (4), pressed.compound = Back/View (8), edge-triggered with the same 500 ms resync. Raw layout: RB 5 / B 1, LB 4, Back 6. The new buttons count for `active`. mergeInput(keys, out) now also sets out.boost = !!keys.boost || pad boost, and out.limiter = !!keys.limiter (the pad never toggles the limiter itself).
 - WHAT main.js MUST DO. Keys and pad: (1) keep a limiter toggle flipped by Q (keydown, not repeat) and by pad.state.pressed.limiter, and put it in keys.limiter before mergeInput. (2) Set keys.boost while E is held. (3) T or pressed.compound selects the next compound (F1.Tyres.nextCompound). Without a pad, pass {..., boost, limiter} to car.update directly.
 - WHAT main.js MUST DO. Car, tyres, battery: (4) car.setBattery(1) on track load, at the start of qualifying and on the grid (reset() does not recharge). (5) car.tyres.fit(compound) on those occasions and at pit 'serviceDone'; car.tyres.setWearRate(view.wear) during a session, 1 otherwise; use `if (car.tyres)` guards if a page might lack js/tyres.js. (6) On a car change: car.setSpec(spec), F1.buildRaceLine(track, car.perf), F1.audio.setEngine(car.spec), cockpit.setCar(car.spec, ...).
 - WHAT main.js MUST DO. Display: (7) the HUD and telemetry should read car.state.gear (-1 = R, 0 = N) instead of main.js's own GEAR_KMH / gearFor table, plus state.rpm (with car.spec.rpmIdle/rpmShift/rpmMax), throttle, brake, battery (null when !car.spec.ers), deploy, harvest, limiter, inPit, state.limitKmh, and car.tyres.state for the tyres. (8) Pass car.state to audio.update (all the contract's fields are there). While the car is frozen (grid / service) car.update is not called, so the v6 state fields keep their last values.
