== apiNotes
 - F1.buildTrack(trackData, opts?): opts.year = the session's season. Only the pit limit and its painted signs depend on it.
 - track.locate(x, z, hintIndex, y) and track.nearest(x, z, y): when y is given and the point is at a grade-separated crossing (Suzuka), they return the road whose surface at (x, z) is closest to y. The other road is a candidate only if (x, z) is inside its corridor. Without y (undefined) the behaviour is unchanged. track.groundY(x, z, y) works the same way. track.inCorridor(x, z, margin) is true inside either level's corridor.
 - track.bridges = [{up, lo, separation, deckFrom, deckTo, deckLength, clearance, abutmentSegments}]. up / lo = the sample of the upper / lower road at the crossing. track.crossings is unchanged ([[1160, 2345]] at Suzuka).
 - Pit limits: track.pit.limitFor(year) gives the km/h for that season, never above pit.layoutLimitKmh (what the lane geometry allows). track.pit.setYear(year) sets pit.limitKmh, switches the painted signs and returns the limit. pit.year is the season limitKmh is for (null = the current layout's rule). F1.pitLimitFor(trackData, year) gives the data's limit without the geometry cap. Defaults with no year: Zandvoort 80, Singapore 80, Sochi 60, Monaco 60.
 - Glue for main.js (other owner): build with F1.buildTrack(data, {year: active season}), or call track.pit.setYear(year) whenever the session season changes. js/pit.js caches state.limitKmh when it binds a track, so call setYear before that bind or reset / rebind pit after it. car.js and main.js read pit.limitKmh live.
 - New trackData fields (tracks-data.js): pitLimits [{from?, to?, kmh}] (season years, inclusive; nl-1948 and sg-2008 = [{to: 2024, kmh: 60}]), widthOverrides [{name, from, to, halfW}] (lap fractions; az-2016), bankMaxDeg (1.5 on 10 street circuits), layout (pt-1972 note). pitSide -1 is added for gb-1948 and pitLimitKmh 60 for ru-2014. Singapore's pitLimitKmh field is removed: its current limit is 80.
 - js/track.js: the derived banking cap is BANK_MAX_DEG 2.5 (was 6), overridable per track with trackData.bankMaxDeg. widthOverrides cap the road half width (eased back out over 40 m) and put the walls WALL_ROAD_MARGIN (1 m) outside the road edge (eased over 50 m), so Baku's castle is 7.6 m of road with walls 9.6 m apart.
 - On tracks with per-season limits (Zandvoort, Singapore) the 'paint' mesh now has geometry groups and an ARRAY of materials named 'paint', 'pitSign60' and 'pitSign80'; the inactive sign's material has visible = false. The child mesh count stays at 6. dispose() already handles material arrays.
 - tools/build-tracks.mjs new options: --out <file> writes elsewhere; --check builds in memory and compares with tracks-data.js (exit 1 when different). New tables in the file: LAYOUT_PATCHES, LENGTH_OVERRIDE, LAYOUT_NOTE, WIDTH_OVERRIDES, STREET_CIRCUITS, ELEV_SETTINGS, MANUAL_DIPS. New sources: walmnt, erdtm, ignes, tinitaly, toscana, glo30. BANKED / widthOverrides ends given against the racing direction are now swapped (track.js used to drop them silently).

== summary
I finished the slope and banking presentation in js/cockpit.js and checked it by running it. `node devtests/cockpit-test/api.test.js` gives ALL PASS (71 checks). One harness I do not own now fails: `devtests/gamepad-test/cockpit.test.js`. It needs a one-line change, which I tested on a copy (see openIssues).

What changed in the cockpit:
1. **Banking (cockpit.diff applied as written).** The head takes out 45 % of the car's roll and follows it 0.25 s late. The counter-rotation is about the car's forward axis even when the driver looks aside, and camera.rotation.y is still exactly PI + head yaw.
2. **Compression.** The eye sinks 1.2 cm and the head nods 0.004 rad per g. The value comes from car.state.compress (the physics owner has now added it), or state.seatG - 1 if compress is missing; anything else counts as 0. It is clamped to -1..2.5 g and smoothed over 0.08 s.
3. **Slope (new).** The head slowly takes out 50 % of the car's pitch (time constant 0.6 s), turning about the car's lateral axis. A change of slope still swings the view at first: 0.1 s after a step to a 15 % climb the view has followed 92 % of it. It then settles at 50 %, so a steady climb shows the nose and the road rising against the horizon.
4. **FOV.** Default is 60 deg vertical; `cockpit.setFov(deg)` takes 50..75 (non-numbers give 60) and applies at once. The speed widening stays proportional: 60 goes to 70.3 deg at top speed, and 70 goes to 82 exactly as v5.
5. **Framing (my own addition).** With FOV 60 alone, the steering-wheel display slid under the HUD's bottom telemetry graphic; I saw this in the HUD-on screenshots. A vertical lens shift in the projection matrix keeps the display's lower edge where v5 drew it at the same speed. It does not tilt the view and does not touch camera.view. At FOV 60 the horizon sits at 43 % of the screen height, so more road is visible; setting 70 means no shift at all.

On a level road with no compression at setFov(70), the camera is bit-identical to the frozen v5 cockpit: pose, FOV and projection all match (maxDiff 0).

== apiNotes
 - **New cockpit call:** `cockpit.setFov(deg)` returns the value it applied.
- It sets the vertical FOV at a standstill and clamps it to 50..75.
- Numeric strings are accepted; NaN, null, undefined, objects, Infinity and booleans give the default 60.
- It applies at once and the speed widening scales with it.
- For the glue agent: in the 設定 tab, add a 視野 slider (F1.COCKPIT_FOV.min..max, default .def), save it, and call `cockpit.setFov(saved)` right after `F1.createCockpit` and whenever the slider moves.
- Guard the call with `if (cockpit.setFov)`: the createCockpit stub in test/main.test.js has no setFov.
 - **New constants:**
- `F1.COCKPIT_FOV = { def: 60, min: 50, max: 75, widen: 12/70 }`.
- `F1.COCKPIT_HEAD = { rollKeep 0.45, rollTau 0.25 s, pitchKeep 0.5, pitchTau 0.6 s, compressEye 0.012 m/g, compressNod 0.004 rad/g, compressMin -1, compressMax 2.5, compressTau 0.08 s }`.
- `cockpit.info()` now also returns `fov: { setting, now, shift }` and `head: { roll, pitch, compress }`.
 - **What the cockpit reads from car.state:**
- It now reads `compress` (g, the road-shape share of the load: +1.8 at T3, +1.6 at Eau Rouge, below 0 over crests).
- If compress is missing it uses `seatG - 1`, where seatG must be the seat load in g WITHOUT downforce. Never pass car.state.load as seatG: load includes downforce and would sink the eye by 3 cm all the time at speed.
- Pitch and roll are read through a finiteness guard.
 - **Camera behaviour:**
- `camera.rotation.y` is still exactly PI + head yaw (main.js's audio listener and gp-smoke's pad checks still pass).
- `rotation.x` and `rotation.z` now carry the counter-roll, counter-pitch and nod.
- The cockpit model (group) still follows the car one-to-one, identical to v5.
- Calling `cockpit.update(state, 0)`, as main.js does after a track load, sets the head's smoothed roll, pitch and compression to their current values at once.
 - **Framing shift:**
- Only `camera.projectionMatrix.elements[9]` (and its inverse) is changed; `camera.view` is not used.
- `cockpit.update` re-applies the shift every frame while the FOV setting is not 70.
- An outside `camera.updateProjectionMatrix()` (main.js on resize, or harnesses that borrow the camera for overview shots: grid-shots, pit-test, track-fix, bank-shots) gets a plain centred projection.
- The extra cost is about 0.001 ms per update (cockpit.update 0.0032-0.0047 ms).
 - **Text for js/README-interfaces.md** (the doc owner should add it to the `js/cockpit.js + js/carmodel.js` section):
- `setFov(deg)` and `F1.COCKPIT_FOV` / `F1.COCKPIT_HEAD`.

== apiNotes

 - js/scenery.js runtime rules (text for README-interfaces, js/scenery.js section): (a) a mapped deck over the road is not built where an inside sample lies within 12 m of the upper road of track.bridges[k] (deckFrom..deckTo), i.e. the lap's own bridge at Suzuka, or of a covered stretch of F1.TUNNEL_DATA[trackData.id] (when js/tunnels.js is loaded; located through trackData.geo and fFrom / fTo).
